Course work is easy to forget after it is submitted. Turning it into a readable log is not about copying a report to a website; it is about retaining the context behind the decisions.

## A useful structure

### A one-sentence brief

State the task, platform, and most important outcome. A reader should understand the work in under a minute.

### Evidence from the process

Include key sketches, parameters, experimental comparisons, and implementation excerpts. Failed attempts are useful too: they explain why the final approach was chosen over the alternatives.

### Verifiable deliverables

Link the repository, report, demo video, or runnable build. When relevant, describe the data and environment needed to reproduce the result.

### A next step

List the questions that remain unresolved. This turns a submitted assignment into the starting point for the next project.

Future entries will follow this structure where possible, while adding the material each course needs: images, models, user studies, or real-time demonstrations.
