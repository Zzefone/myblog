This is not a website that only displays end-of-term outcomes. It is a living record of work in progress.

Each course page will collect its assignments and experiments; each work log will focus on one concrete question. To make later reflection easier, I will use a consistent structure:

1. **Task** — What is the problem and what are its constraints?
2. **Method** — Which models, tools, or design decisions were used?
3. **Implementation** — What are the essential steps and version choices?
4. **Result** — What do screenshots, metrics, demos, or failures show?
5. **Reflection** — Which assumptions held up, and what should change next time?

The course pages are ready; they will be filled as new work is completed.
